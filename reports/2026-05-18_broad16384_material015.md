## Conclusion

This branch is rejected. The larger broad MultiPV replay set made the candidate
drawish against the current best but weaker on the fixed Stockfish diagnostic
and still losing to Stockfish directly. More broad low-depth teacher positions
are not the next useful lever by themselves; the next branch should use more
targeted tactical/value supervision or improve search rather than increasing
the broad replay weight.
